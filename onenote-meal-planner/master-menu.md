Pasta & Italian Classics
- Spaghetti Bolognese (Spag Bol) – Long-standing staple prepared with lean beef mince, canned tomatoes, and spaghetti.
- Lasagne – Baked pasta dish featured regularly for family dinners and weekend gatherings.
- Pasta Alla Vodka / Penne Alla Vodka – Penne pasta cooked in a creamy tomato-vodka sauce.
- Spaghetti Alla Puttanesca – Spaghetti tossed with tomatoes, olives, capers, garlic, and anchovies/herbs.
- Orecchiette Sausage Pasta – Orecchiette pasta tossed with pork sausage in a creamy tomato sauce.
- Four Cheese Ravioli / Tortellini – Quick fresh or frozen filled pasta tossed with tomato, capsicum, capers, or pesto.
- Cheesy Chicken & Roasted Tomato Pasta Bake – Penne baked with chicken breast, passata, and melted mozzarella.
- Ricotta & Spinach Pasta Bake – Vegetarian pasta bake made with spinach, ricotta, mozzarella, and passata.
- Macaroni & Cheese (and Veggie / Chicken Mac) – Classic comfort bake prepared with macaroni, cheese sauce, and optional veggies or chicken.
- Pesto Pasta / Red Pesto Pasta – Spaghetti or penne tossed with basil or sun-dried tomato pesto and chicken.
- Creamy Mustard Chicken Pasta – Chicken thigh cooked with cream, tarragon, bacon, and wholegrain mustard over pasta.
- Chicken & Bacon Creamy Pasta – Rigatoni or penne cooked with cream, chicken thigh, bacon, and mushrooms.
- Creamy Garlic Prawn Pasta / Prawn Linguini – Linguini tossed with garlic prawns, cream, and parmesan.
- Carbonara / Spaghetti & Meatballs / Meatball Subs – Classic pasta favorites and sub rolls stuffed with meatballs and melted mozzarella.

Asian & Asian-Inspired Mains
- Asian Beef Bowls – Fast 15-minute stir-fry of beef mince, ginger, garlic, soy, and sesame oil served over jasmine rice.
- Vietnamese Rice Paper Rolls (Cold Rolls) – Fresh rice paper wrapped with prawns, chicken, or pork, vermicelli noodles, herbs, and cucumber.
- Vietnamese Bun Cha (Pork Meatball Noodle Bowls) – Char-grilled pork meatballs served over vermicelli noodles, pickled carrots, cucumber, and fresh herbs with dipping sauce.
- Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken / Pork Noodle Bowls (Bun Tit Nuong) – Tender lemongrass-marinated chicken or pork steaks served over vermicelli and crisp salad greens.
- Teriyaki Chicken / Salmon Bowls – Pan-seared chicken or salmon glazed in teriyaki sauce, served over rice with cucumber and avocado.
- Korean Beef Bulgogi – Thinly sliced marinated beef cooked with pear, soy, ginger, and sesame oil over rice.
- Japchae (Korean Glass Noodles) – Sweet potato glass noodles stir-fried with beef, shiitake mushrooms, spinach, carrots, and sesame.
- Taiwanese Three-Cup Chicken – Chicken thighs braised with soy, sesame oil, rice wine, garlic, ginger, and fresh basil.
- Thai Beef Salad / Thai Chicken Salad – Seared beef or chicken tossed with cucumber, cherry tomatoes, mint, coriander, and a tangy chilli-lime dressing.
- Thai Green Curry / Thai Red Curry – Chicken or beef simmered with coconut milk, curry paste, bamboo shoots, and green vegetables over rice.
- Hokkien Noodles / Pad See Ew / Drunken Noodles – Hokkien or flat rice noodles stir-fried with chicken or pork, greens, and savory soy sauces.
- Laksa Noodle Soup / Dumpling Noodle Soup – Fragrant coconut laksa or clear chicken stock broth served with dumplings, noodles, and bok choy.
- Fried Rice / Nasi Goreng / Jollof Rice – Classic egg and bacon fried rice, Indonesian spicy fried rice, or West African spiced Jollof rice.
- Beef & Black Bean Stir-Fry / Garlic Pepper Beef – Rump steak stir-fried with black bean sauce or garlic-pepper oyster sauce and snow peas.

Mexican & Tex-Mex
- Tacos (Beef, Chicken, Fish, Pork Tinga, Prawn & Chorizo) – Soft tortillas or hard shells filled with seasoned minces, shredded chicken, or seafood with avocado salsa and lime.
- Quesadillas (Cheese & Bean, Beef, Chicken, Steak) – Toasted tortillas filled with spiced meat, black beans, corn, and melted cheese.
- Burrito Bowls / Burritos – Rice bowls layered with seasoned beef, black beans, corn, avocado, tomatoes, and sour cream.
- Chilli Con Carne / Baked Potatoes – Slow-simmered beef mince with kidney beans and spices, served over rice, corn chips, or fluffy baked potatoes.
- Enchiladas / Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas – Baked tortilla wraps or pan-seared chicken strips with capsicum, onions, and fajita spices.

Burgers, Gyros, Wraps & Sandwiches
- Smash Burgers / Beef Burgers – Pan-seared beef patties topped with cheese slices, lettuce, tomato, and burger sauce on brioche buns.
- Halloumi Burgers / Halloumi BLT Burgers – Pan-fried halloumi slices with avocado, lettuce, tomato, and bacon or sweet chilli mayo on potato buns.
- Greek Chicken Gyros / Lamb Yiros / Kofta Pitas – Marinated chicken or lamb folded into pita wraps with tzatziki, lettuce, tomato, cucumber, and feta.
- Steak Sandwiches – Thinly sliced steak, caramelised onions, lettuce, tomato, and mustard served on toasted sourdough or rolls.
- BLTs / BLT Salad – Crisp bacon, lettuce, tomato, and avocado served in toasted rolls or tossed as a salad with balsamic dressing.
- Tandoori Chicken Wraps / Kebabs & Salad – Marinated chicken wrapped in flatbreads with yogurt sauce and fresh salad.

Vegetarian & Grain-Based Mains
- Crispy Zucchini Fritters – Grated zucchini and spring onions bound with flour and eggs, fried golden and served with garlic-herb yogurt dip and greens.
- Bill Granger's Corn Fritters with Avocado Salsa – Sweet corn fritters served with fresh tomato and avocado salsa.
- Roasted Cauliflower Orzo with Chickpeas & Feta – Roasted cauliflower florets and chickpeas tossed with tender orzo, crumbled feta, lemon, and parsley.
- Creamy Goat Cheese & Roasted Red Pepper Orzo – Risoni pasta cooked with jarred red peppers, spinach, passata, and creamy goat cheese.
- Syrian Chicken with Pearl/Giant Couscous – Spiced chicken thighs braised and served with fluffy pearl couscous, lemon, and yogurt.
- Couscous Stuffed Capsicum – Roasted bell peppers stuffed with seasoned couscous, herbs, and feta.
- Middle Eastern Spiced Chickpea Salad / Lamb & Chickpea Salad – Spiced chickpeas, roasted peppers, spinach, and feta tossed in a lemon-tahini dressing.
- Shakshuka with Feta & Sourdough – Eggs poached in a spiced tomato, capsicum, and onion sauce topped with crumbled feta.
- Lentil Curry / Tomato Lentil Soup – Brown or red lentils simmered with curry spices, tomato, and coconut milk served with rice or pita.

Poultry, Roast & Meat Mains
- Butter Chicken – Tender chicken thighs simmered in a rich tomato, butter, and spiced cream sauce served with naan and basmati rice.
- Chicken Kievs & Roast Potatoes / Veg – Garlic-butter stuffed chicken kievs baked and served with mash or roasted baby potatoes and broccoli.
- Steak & Veg / Steak & Smash Potatoes – Rump or strip steaks seared and served with roasted baby potatoes, broccolini, and gravy.
- Shepherd's Pie / Beef Bourguignon / Beef Stroganoff – Hearty beef mince or chuck beef stew topped with mashed potato or served over egg noodles.
- Roast Meats (Lamb, Pork, Chicken, Duck) – Slow-roasted meats served with roast potatoes, carrots, pumpkin, and gravy.
- Slow-Cooker Massaman Lamb Shoulder / Moroccan Lamb – Fall-apart lamb shoulder braised with curry paste, coconut milk, and baby potatoes.
- Chicken with Creamy Mushroom Sauce (Jamon Chicken) – Pan-seared chicken breast or thigh topped with a rich garlic mushroom cream sauce.
- Chicken Mornay Pie / Chicken & Bacon Pie – Shredded chicken, sweet corn, leeks, and cream wrapped in golden puff pastry.
- Bangers & Mash / One-Pan Sausage Tray Bake – Pork or beef sausages baked with potatoes, tomatoes, and caramelised onions topped with gravy.

Seafood & Fish
- Garlic Prawns (with Rice or Broccolini) – Sautéed prawns in garlic, butter, and cream or white wine served over rice or pasta.
- Lemon Garlic Salmon Tray Bake – Salmon fillets baked on a single tray with baby potatoes, asparagus, lemon, and garlic oil.
- Pan-Fried Whiting / Barramundi Fillets – White fish fillets pan-seared with lemon butter or baked in greaseproof paper (en papillote).
- Seafood Chowder / Scallop Curry – Creamy fish, prawn, and calamari chowder or tender scallops cooked in a mild curry sauce.

Salads, Soups & Light Meals
- Honey Mustard Chicken Salad – Grilled chicken breast, bacon, avocado, cherry tomatoes, and mixed greens with honey-mustard dressing.
- Grilled Lemon Herb Mediterranean Chicken Salad – Marinated chicken tossed with cucumber, tomatoes, olives, feta, and lemon oregano dressing.
- Prawn & Avocado / Corn Salad – Sautéed garlic prawns tossed with sweet corn cobs, ripe avocados, cherry tomatoes, and lime dressing.
- Blood Orange & Fennel Salad – Thinly sliced fennel, blood orange segments, and mint tossed with olive oil and served with pork or salmon.
- Cob Salad / Caesar Salad – Crisp lettuce layered with chicken, bacon, boiled eggs, avocado, and creamy Caesar or ranch dressing.
- Smoky Roasted Tomato Soup & Cheese Toasties – Slow-roasted vine tomatoes blended with basil and garlic, served alongside grilled cheese toasties.
- Potato & Leek Soup – Smooth blended potato and leek soup made with chicken stock, cream, and chives.
